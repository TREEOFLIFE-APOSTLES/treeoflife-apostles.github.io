Warm facts: Beethoven lived 1770–1827. The “Moonlight” Sonata (Op. 27 No. 2) was composed in 1801 — its nickname came later, from a poet who heard moonlight on a lake in its opening triplets. Für Elise was written in his lifetime but only published in 1867, forty years after his death, when its manuscript was rediscovered. Tonight we transcribe both famous openings ourselves from the public-domain compositions — no recordings, no samples.
