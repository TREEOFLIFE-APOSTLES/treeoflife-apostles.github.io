Pointer stars: Dubhe and Merak, the two outer stars of the bowl, are called the Pointers — a line drawn through them points toward Polaris, the North Star. In the real sky Polaris sits about five Merak→Dubhe distances beyond Dubhe; here that distance is lovingly compressed so the whole journey fits one canvas. Follow the pointers home.

A double star: Mizar, the middle star of the handle, has a tiny companion, Alcor, riding beside it (look for the little dot once Mizar lights). Sharp eyes have used the Mizar–Alcor pair as an eyesight test for centuries; they shine together in our sky too.

Facts in one line each: Dubhe and Merak guard the bowl's outer edge · Phecda sits at the bowl's lower inner corner · Megrez, the faintest bowl star, joins bowl to handle · Alioth begins the handle's arc · Mizar bends the handle · Alkaid (Benetnasch) tips the handle's end.
