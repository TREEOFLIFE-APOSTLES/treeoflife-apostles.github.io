🐉 Dragon Curve Treasure

How to play — controls

Walk the Curve starts the game. A tiny Earth Dragon walks along an unfolding dragon curve, fold by fold, from iteration 1 to 12. Glowing lanterns appear on the curve's corners — tap or click a lantern before it fades to catch it for +10. A missed lantern simply fades away: no fail, no death, no penalty. Catch lanterns in a quick chain for the house 3-6-9 combo: every 3rd in a chain is ×3, every 6th is ×6, and every 9th bursts “589!” at ×9. Every completed fold lights the walked path from teal to gold. Pause with the button or P.

Explore mode — just watch it grow

Tap Explore Mode, or move the fold slider (1–12) at any time, and the game pauses into watching: the fractal simply grows, fold by fold, with no lanterns and no score. Move the slider back and forth to see a single paper fold become 4,096 segments.

Win

Reach iteration 12 having caught 30 lanterns and the treasure opens: “Dragon Fully Unfolded” with fireworks. If you finish the walk with fewer, keep exploring or walk again — the dragon is patient.

What is the Heighway dragon curve?

Imagine a strip of paper. Fold it in half, again and again, always in the same direction, then open it so every crease is a right angle. The edge you get is the Heighway dragon curve, named for NASA physicist John Heighway and popularised by Martin Gardner. It never crosses itself, yet it fills more and more of the plane, and it can tile the plane with copies of itself. A giant version famously folded through the chapter headings of Michael Crichton's Jurassic Park. Here our Earth Dragon walks it as treasure: each fold doubles the path, and every corner can hold a lantern.

Sound

Sound is off by default. Turn it on for gentle pentatonic chimes — a soft note on a catch, and a rising note as each fold completes. Low volume, and it stops when you turn it off.
