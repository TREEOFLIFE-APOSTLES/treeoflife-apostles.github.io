🐉 Dragon Flight · Earth Dragon

How to play

Hold (touch, mouse, Space or ↑ ArrowUp) to rise, release to glide down. A simple tap-flap works too — tap, tap, tap gently. Collect golden lanterns and stars for +10. There is nothing harmful here: no enemies, no death, no falling out of the sky. Passing through clouds, snow gusts or lava smoke only slows you in a soft slow sparkle. The sky slowly speeds up as you fly, and your best score is kept on this device only.

3-6-9 combo: collect lanterns quickly in a row — the 3rd gives ×3, the 6th gives ×6 sparkle, and the 9th bursts into a golden 589! fireworks sparkle. Pause with the button or P.

Three biomes Father chose

🌊 Ocean — waves below, Pisces fish swimming by, and faint family names falling like our Digital Waterfall name-rain. ❄️ Snow — falling snow over white peaks and soft gusts. 🌋 Lava — dark rocks, an orange glow, and embers rising — beautiful from a safe, gentle distance. The biomes cycle as you fly.

Sound

Sound is off by default. Turn it on for a soft singing-bowl chime on each lantern (low volume) and a gentle wind whoosh when the biome changes. The dragon's tail leaves a sparkle trail, and lanterns burst into brief Chladni-ish star patterns.
