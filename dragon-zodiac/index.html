🐉 Dragon Zodiac Sky

How to play — controls

Tap / click the sky, or press Space, to launch the dragon inward from its orbit. The Earth Dragon auto-orbits the zodiac wheel on its own between dives. Catch the falling star of the target sign shown at the top for +18 and that constellation lights up. Catching a wrong sign only sparkles — no penalty, no death. Pause with the button or P. Your best score is kept on this device only.

Timer: Endless free-play is on by default. Untick “Endless free-play” before you start for a gentle 90-second attempt — when time ends, the sky simply rests and you can start again.

Win and the Pisces bonus

Light all 12 constellations to win “Whole Sky Lit” with fireworks. Then the final bonus round begins: catch Pisces 3 times. ♓ Pisces is highlighted in gold as our house sign — the two fish, Father’s sign in this house story, and the gold constellation on the wheel.
