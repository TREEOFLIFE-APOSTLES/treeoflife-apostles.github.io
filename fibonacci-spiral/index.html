🌿 The golden ratio ≈ 1.618
Divide each Fibonacci number by the one before it: 3÷2 = 1.5, 5÷3 ≈ 1.667, 8÷5 = 1.6, 13÷8 = 1.625, 21÷13 ≈ 1.615… The answers creep ever closer to φ ≈ 1.618, the golden ratio. Spiral counts in many sunflower heads and pine cones tend to be neighbouring Fibonacci numbers, like 34 and 55.

“The tree which fills the arms grew from the tiniest sprout.” — Laozi, Tao Te Ching ch. 64, James Legge translation (1891).

How to play: Press Start, then Grow — the squares add one by one (1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21), each labelled with its number. When all eight stand, the golden spiral draws itself through them, then 13 petals and a seed head bloom at its heart (13 is a Fibonacci number too, and seeds in nature often pack at the golden angle, about 137.5°). Use One square to savour it step by step, Slower / Faster to change the pace, and Replay to grow it again. No timer, no fail.
