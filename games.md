## Shapeshifter: Tree of Life

[▶ Play now](https://xrplove.github.io/shapeshifter-game/){: .btn .btn-primary } [Source](https://github.com/xrplove/shapeshifter-game){: .btn }

Grow through five forms, each with its own power and lesson:

1. **Ant** — carry 5 crumbs home to the nest.
2. **Fly** — fly up over the walls to reach the flower.
3. **Vampire** — use night sight to find 3 lost fireflies in the dark.
4. **Angel** — heal 5 wilted flowers.
5. **King or Queen** — you choose! Gather the whole family home to the Tree of Life.

Arrow keys and Space on a computer, tap buttons on a phone. Music and sounds are made in code; the 🔊 button mutes.
