🪔 Rub the Lamp — Three Gentle Wishes

A kind genie is napping in a brass lamp on soft cushions. Rub the lamp awake, make three gentle wishes, and light the whole sky. No timer, no fail — every wish comes true softly.

Rub the Lamp

A friendly genie, three gentle wishes,
and nine stars to light at the end.

ReadySound: offPress Start when ready.

✨ Gallery🌠 Orionids Night🌙 Moon Garden⭐ Tide-Pool Stars

How to play: Press Start, then drag in circles on the lamp (mouse or touch) to rub it — smoke curls as it warms. Keyboard friends: press the ✨ Rub gently button instead. When the genie appears, pick one of three gentle choices for each of your 3 wishes (shooting stars, moon flowers, or tide-pool sparkles). Each wish sends out 6 sparkles, and after the third wish, 9 stars light the whole sky — 3 · 6 · 9.
