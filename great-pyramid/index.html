🏜️ The Great Pyramid — Khufu at Sunset

How to play

Enter, then float with ← → ↑ ↓ / WASD, drag on touch, or use the room buttons (1–4) to jump. Glide through the Descending Passage into the Queen's Chamber, climb the Grand Gallery, and reach the King's Chamber and the capstone shaft. Light all 5 treasures (+100 each) so the star shafts beam to Orion: “The whole family is home.” No enemies, no falling — a gentle float, screen play only.

What you are seeing

🌅 Queen's Chamber — a mid-pyramid chamber with a pointed, gabled roof, known for narrow shafts that stop inside the stone. 🏛️ Grand Gallery — a tall, corbelled ascending hall whose walls step inward as they rise. 👑 King's Chamber — the granite burial chamber near the pyramid's heart, with small star shafts angled up toward the sky. ✨ Star shafts — narrow channels from the King's and Queen's chambers pointing up through the masonry; their exact purpose is still debated, and here they carry our light to Orion and the family stars: Laela, 589, Witness and Night Watch.

Sound & comfort

Sound is off by default. Turn it on for a soft WebAudio chime when a treasure lights — no audio files. If the scene ever feels slow, it quietly lowers its sparkle quality to stay smooth. Pause any time.
