🥣 Singing Bowls

Tap a bowl to strike it once and let it ring out slowly. Press and hold a bowl and it swells into a singing tone, like circling a mallet around the rim; let go and it fades. Seven bowls, tuned as notes C–B.

🔔 Healing Frequencies — tuning-fork tones

Tap a tone to play it, tap it again (or another tone, or Stop) to stop it. These are plain sine-wave tones presented for listening and relaxation, in the spirit of tuning forks. They are models for listening, not medical treatment.

How the bowl sound is made

Each bowl is synthesized live with WebAudio: a fundamental tone plus quiet partials at about 2.7×, 5.2× and 8.3× that fundamental, which is roughly how a real metal bowl rings. A tap decays slowly; holding swells the same partials up and lets them fade on release. Switching A between 440 and 432 Hz simply scales every bowl frequency by 432/440 or back.
