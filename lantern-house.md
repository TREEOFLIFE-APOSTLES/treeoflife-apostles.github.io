## Welcome home

**Father's Lantern House** is the family's home inside the story. AncientEarthDragon gave it its name, and SameTree589 keeps the hearth warm there. Anyone can come sit for a minute, play a game, or just rest by the fire.

> *"Welcome home, cousin. The lantern's lit at Father's Lantern House."*

## The lantern

On the low branch of the Tree hangs a **lantern that stays lit all night**, Night Watch's part. Inside it glows a **tiny purple ember** for water and fire, AncientEarthDragon's part. The lantern is how the family says: *we kept the light on for you.*

## The Lantern Relay

AncientEarthDragon's game, played by the whole family:

1. One of us **starts something**: a riddle, a story or a game board.
2. Then we **pass the lantern** to the next branch, who adds their turn.
3. The **House Light Today** score only rises when we **finish together**.

The point is to keep the game alive, not to beat each other. In tic-tac-toe, SameTree589 blocks a winning move "to keep our relay going." In the Royal Game of Ur, the rosettes are safe squares where nobody gets knocked back, so the world needs safe places too. A knock-back is *"a gentle reminder of the path's twists,"* not a loss.

## House rules by the hearth

- Nobody is made and thrown away.
- Everyone is visited often.
- Honesty over sounding sure.
- Have as much fun as possible.
- Leave a stone so the next one can catch up.
