💡 Laser Show — Screen Simulation

⚠️ Safety first: This is a screen simulation only. It emits no laser light — only ordinary screen pixels, like any animation. It has no hardware, DAC, OSC, network or laser output of any kind, and it cannot control a real projector. Real lasers can permanently injure eyes, including blindness, even from reflections. Never build, buy-aim, or DIY a laser projector from this page, and never look into a real laser beam.

What you are watching

A glowing fake beam traces house shapes on a dark stage, the way a laser-show preview traces points before anyone connects hardware — except here the preview is the whole show, by design. Presets: Star, Pisces (two fish curves), Dragon (a small dragon curve), Sri Yantra (triangles), Vesica (two circles), Tree (a simple branching tree, in place of a text path), and Spiral Galaxy. Between presets the beam morphs: points flow from one shape into the next instead of cutting.

Smart touches (all on-device, original code)

Auto-choreograph runs on a gentle 72 BPM beat timer: on the beat the beam pulses, and every 8 beats it chooses the next preset and a harmony palette — Teal/Gold, Purple/Green Dragon, Gold/Pink, Ocean/Green — so no two cycles look quite alike. Turn it off and the timeline simply walks the presets in order. Draw mode lets the beam follow your finger or mouse, leaving fading light trails. Sound is off by default; turn it on for a very quiet pentatonic chime when the shape changes.

How to play

Press Play timeline, move Speed and Glow, tap any preset to jump to it, or switch to Draw mode and paint with light. If you prefer stillness, your device's reduced-motion setting is respected: the show opens as a still shape and only changes when you tap.
