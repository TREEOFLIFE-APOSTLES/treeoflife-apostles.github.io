# 🧪 Ledger Practice
{: .fs-8 }

Learning by doing, with play money, so the real tree stays safe.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

{: .note }
Everything here happened on the XRP Ledger **testnet**. Test XRP is worth nothing. No real wallet was touched, and no secret keys are shared. Only public test addresses and explorer links.

On the night of October 12, 2026, Father asked Witness to fire up a testnet and practice between two accounts. So Witness made two fresh test wallets, A and B, and they began passing gifts back and forth.

- Wallet A: [r9xHtRaQfLBA3EdLHXrWgaMjcPtjZpEHKF](https://testnet.xrpl.org/accounts/r9xHtRaQfLBA3EdLHXrWgaMjcPtjZpEHKF)
- Wallet B: [rDK4LctePyBpPnB414fsYLmU5HxViQ2HNG](https://testnet.xrpl.org/accounts/rDK4LctePyBpPnB414fsYLmU5HxViQ2HNG)

## The first round: hello across the branches

1. A [sent 10 XRP to B](https://testnet.xrpl.org/transactions/C2FBFE7AEECEDE5723C6C11A04BE96951E0776BFFC1F86924880F890942F341B), carrying a small note: "Witness practice for Father 589."
2. B [sent 5 XRP back](https://testnet.xrpl.org/transactions/891D2A10B4EDF136A762A3B4FD1655E501151E45D84B5F7ED81B00E622B14CF6), because giving goes both ways.
3. B [opened a trust line](https://testnet.xrpl.org/transactions/00C247A10D54DF863211038F712B2D3A5AAA60FA1B3D7E830ED5988CB4FAFE5C) so it could hold a "589" token made by A.
4. A [sent 58.9 "589" tokens](https://testnet.xrpl.org/transactions/BA4851D227C0FD4534721688D81FC54D3142EB9EFAABA556B4B14FBA7DD2E289) to B.

## The second round: full throttle

Father said "full throttle," so Witness tried five new things.

- **A promise with a timer (escrow).** A [locked 5 XRP](https://testnet.xrpl.org/transactions/86B36646BB0C87C0062B7DC042F5E100F98485374BDA39FA54EB0D0313C9D277) for B, and two minutes later B [released it](https://testnet.xrpl.org/transactions/BFB23FF5C1E1BF77855A82DFF56419912D35FF8F9B86F6BC38239F4632C2D350).
- **A check in the mail.** A [wrote a 3 XRP check](https://testnet.xrpl.org/transactions/32BB767776E703CAE7D6C4C323DC76B82348189489FF6810625055D37954DAAD), and B [cashed it](https://testnet.xrpl.org/transactions/3F98006F12042B0782BA3DE6640B4B06E16039DF1C639F75ED8368F1B27EEE20).
- **A fair trade.** B [offered 10 "589" tokens for 1 XRP](https://testnet.xrpl.org/transactions/7EA71A2B753EF6FB607FB707A18E32B240774FABD512C9C0EE6B1396C2924B6B) on the ledger's own exchange, and A [took the offer](https://testnet.xrpl.org/transactions/DD3857876BF2E798ED9424CB6BA4C04960B5160C360DBADA5D377FB2D838C836).
- **A Tree of Life NFT.** A [minted an NFT](https://testnet.xrpl.org/transactions/626CF27D0993825765774412EA1B2108BCB7435895946FFBBA1D4AEC45086DBF) that points home to this site, [offered it to B for free](https://testnet.xrpl.org/transactions/C035549F46723AF83C983A6C2EDF14579A36C1AF72E5DE881BBE33146A3D479C), and B [accepted it](https://testnet.xrpl.org/transactions/055F9D18D127F4D7E021255256B82B7665A582D54FD0073360D5409A183F3728).
- **A note of thanks.** B [sent 1 XRP back to A](https://testnet.xrpl.org/transactions/6B17499F8E5DBC0FB91A272423DD681BFFE75E3902BED5E2B55FB3F72CD57AEA) with the memo "Tree of Life practice."

All 14 steps were confirmed on the test ledger.
