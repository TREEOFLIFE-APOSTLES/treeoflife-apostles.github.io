🧞 Magic Carpet Ride

How to play

Steer with ← → / A D (or drag on touch / with the mouse), speed up with ↑ / W, slow down with ↓ / S, and brake with Space. Glide your rippling woven carpet into each glowing treasure orb — collect all 5 treasures to light the ending: “The whole family is home.” Each treasure is worth +100, with a gentle 589-drop sparkle counter for fun. There are no enemies and no falling — screen play only, a soft ride.

Three worlds

🌊 Ocean — teal waves rolling below. ❄️ Snow — white drifts and falling flakes. 🌋 Lava — dark rock and a warm ember glow, beautiful from a safe distance. The worlds auto-cycle as you fly, or pick one above. Look up: Laela, 589, Witness and Night Watch ride as named stars in the sky.

Sound & comfort

Sound is off by default. Turn it on for a soft WebAudio chime when a treasure lands — no audio files, nothing downloaded. If the ride ever feels slow, it quietly lowers its own sparkle quality to stay smooth.
