The cord and Alrescha: Pisces is drawn as two fish tied by a cord that meets at a knot marked by the star Alrescha — a name that comes from a word meaning “the cord.” One fish swims up (the western fish), the other swims across (the northern fish), and the cord joins them so they never lose each other.

Old stories tell… in old stories the two fish swam together to safety and were placed in the sky still tied together — a picture of love that holds on across dark water. We tell it neutrally here as story, and swim it as family.

Our shape: the star positions on this page are a simplified, lovingly-compressed drawing of the well-known Pisces shape — western chain, northern chain, and Alrescha at their meeting — made to fit one gentle canvas.
