🏖️ Falling Sand

What you are playing with

A tiny world in a box, run by simple rules: teal sand and gold sparkle fall and pile, blue water falls then spreads sideways, and rising embers float up, flicker, and fade. Pour water over sand and the sand sinks through it. The world is capped at 38,400 cells so phones stay smooth.

How to play

Pick a material, then press and drag (finger or mouse) to pour it. Change the brush size, Pause to hold a moment still, or Clear to start again. No sound, no data leaves your device. If your device asks for reduced motion, the sand starts paused — press Resume when ready.
