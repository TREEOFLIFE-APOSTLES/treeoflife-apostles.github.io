🦋 Shapeshifter: Tree of Life

How to play

Grow through five gentle forms: Ant → Fly → Vampire → Angel → King or Queen. Each form has one power, one goal, and one lesson. Ant carries 5 crumbs home, Fly rises over walls to the flower, Vampire finds 3 fireflies in the dark, Angel heals 5 flowers, and the Crown gathers everyone home to the Tree of Life.

Controls: arrow keys + Space for your power, or the on-screen tap buttons on phones. Space / tap Power also begins and moves to the next form. The 🔊 button (top right) mutes. Sound and art are generated in code; nothing is sent anywhere.
